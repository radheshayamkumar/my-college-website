Why students choose GHRCEM

Three things the college leads with, in the words the department heads actually use.

Core courses pair every theory session with hands-on lab time, so concepts are tested in code and hardware before exams.

Industry-linked projects

Final-year projects are scoped with input from visiting industry mentors and, where possible, real problem statements.
