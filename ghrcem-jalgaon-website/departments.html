Departments & programs

Seven departments across engineering, computer applications and management, each running its own labs, electives and student chapter.

  • 01

    Computer Science & Engineering

    Data structures, systems programming, databases, and electives in AI/ML and applied security.

    B.Tech · 4 yrs
  • 02

    Information Technology

    Web and cloud systems, networking, and enterprise application development.

    B.Tech · 4 yrs
  • 03

    Computer Applications

    Programming foundations through cloud computing, full-stack development and applied project work.

    MCA · 2 yrs
  • 04

    Mechanical Engineering

    Thermal systems, design and manufacturing, with CAD/CAM and workshop practice.

    B.Tech · 4 yrs
  • 05

    Civil Engineering

    Structural design, surveying and construction management, with site-visit components.

    B.Tech · 4 yrs
  • 06

    Electrical Engineering

    Power systems, control systems and electrical machines, with a dedicated instrumentation lab.

    B.Tech · 4 yrs
  • 07

    Management Studies

    Core business functions with a technology-management crossover track for engineering graduates.

    MBA · 2 yrs
