Application process

Four steps from enquiry to enrolment.

  1. Submit an enquiry

    Fill in the form below with your preferred program. The admissions team follows up within a few working days.

  2. Share eligibility documents

    Mark sheets and the relevant entrance exam / merit score, as applicable to your chosen program.

  3. Counselling & seat confirmation

    Attend counselling (institute-level or CAP, depending on the program) and confirm your seat.

  4. Fee payment & enrolment

    Complete fee payment and document verification to finalise enrolment before the term begins.
